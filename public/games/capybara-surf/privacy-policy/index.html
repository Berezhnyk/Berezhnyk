Privacy Policy — Capybara Surf

Last updated: 22 May 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Capybara Surf ("the Game", "we", "us") handles information when you play it on iOS or Android. By installing and playing Capybara Surf you agree to the practices described here.

1. Who we are

Capybara Surf is developed and published by Ivan Berezhnyk ("the Developer"). For any privacy question or request, contact us at ivanberezhnyk@gmail.com.

2. Information we process

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email, or contacts. The Game processes the following limited categories of information:

Categories of information processed by Capybara Surf, with examples and purposes.
CategoryExamplesPurpose
Game progress (stored on your device) Coin balance, best distance/score, owned and equipped cosmetics, settings To save your progress between sessions. This data stays on your device and is not transmitted to us.
Advertising identifiers & ad data Advertising ID (IDFA on iOS / Advertising ID on Android), approximate location derived from IP, device and ad-interaction data To show rewarded and interstitial ads and measure their performance. Collected by Google AdMob (see Section 4).
Purchase information Which in-app product was bought, transaction and receipt identifiers, store country To deliver purchases (coin packs, "Remove Ads", cosmetic bundles), restore them across devices, and prevent fraud. Processed by RevenueCat and the app stores (see Section 4).
Diagnostic & gameplay analytics Anonymous, aggregated events such as a run starting/ending, a shop being opened, or an ad being viewed To understand how the Game is used and improve it. These events are not linked to your identity.

3. How information is used

  • To run the Game and save your progress.
  • To display advertising, including opt-in rewarded ads that grant in-game benefits.
  • To process and restore in-app purchases.
  • To diagnose problems and improve gameplay.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your information to build advertising profiles ourselves; any ad personalization is performed by Google AdMob subject to your consent (see Section 5).

4. Third-party services

Capybara Surf relies on the following third parties, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob — serves in-game advertising. Google Privacy Policy · AdMob & partners.
  • RevenueCat — manages in-app purchases and entitlements. RevenueCat Privacy Policy.
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — process payments for in-app purchases. Apple Privacy Policy · Google Privacy Policy.

5. Your choices and consent

  • iOS App Tracking Transparency (ATT): On first launch, iOS asks whether the Game may track you across apps and websites. If you decline, ads are still shown but are non-personalized.
  • EEA / UK consent: If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, a Google-provided consent form lets you choose whether ads may be personalized. You can reset this choice at any time by reinstalling the Game.
  • Advertising ID: You can reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device settings (iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking / Apple Advertising; Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Remove Ads: The one-time "Remove Ads" purchase disables interstitial ads. Optional rewarded ads remain available if you choose to watch them.

6. Children

Capybara Surf is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Advertising in the Game is configured with a maximum content rating of "Teen" and is not treated as child-directed. If you believe a child under 13 has provided information through the Game, please contact us and we will delete it.

7. Data retention

Game progress is stored only on your device and is removed when you uninstall the Game. Purchase records are retained by RevenueCat and the app stores for as long as needed to support restores and meet legal obligations. Advertising and analytics data is retained by the respective providers under their own policies.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal information, or to object to its processing (for example under the EU/UK GDPR or the California CCPA/CPRA). To exercise these rights, email us at ivanberezhnyk@gmail.com. Because most data is held by Google and RevenueCat, you may also exercise rights directly with them using the links in Section 4.

9. Data security & transfers

We rely on the security measures of Apple, Google, and RevenueCat to protect information processed through their services. These providers may process data in countries other than your own, including the United States, under safeguards described in their respective privacy policies.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the Game evolves. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the Game after a change constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

11. Contact

Questions or requests regarding this Privacy Policy: ivanberezhnyk@gmail.com.
